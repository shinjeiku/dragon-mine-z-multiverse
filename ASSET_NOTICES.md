# Asset notices

## Menu artwork

The God Forms and Ultra Forms menu emblems are original assets created for Dragon Mine Z: Multiverse and are distributed under the project's GPL-3.0-or-later license.

## Custom audio

`god_transform.ogg` and `god_charge.ogg` were converted from MP3 recordings supplied by the project owner. The supplied files did not contain creator, copyright, or license metadata, so the recordings are not currently cleared for public redistribution by this repository.

On August 13, 2026, the project owner confirmed that they are authorized to redistribute both supplied recordings and directed that they be included in the public source repository and binary distributions of Dragon Mine Z: Multiverse. The converted OGG assets are therefore distributed with this project under GPL-3.0-or-later.

The original MP3 working files remain intentionally excluded from Git; only the Minecraft-ready OGG assets are distributed.
